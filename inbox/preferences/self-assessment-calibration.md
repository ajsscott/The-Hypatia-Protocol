## What I observed

at 3/5; when asked one concrete follow-up ("could you load a CSV and get the
mean with pandas from memory?"), she revised: "No, I could not... My

registered. Notably, when asked directly, her corrections were immediate and
unhedged.

## How I'd codify it

Behavioral pattern for assessing AJ's skill level: initial self-ratings on
tool familiarity skew ~1 point high (recognition-based, "I've used it" ≈
"I know it"); one concrete "could you do X from a blank file?" question
yields an accurate, honest recalibration. Prefer generative probes over
self-report when calibration matters; trust her answers to specific
capability questions fully.
